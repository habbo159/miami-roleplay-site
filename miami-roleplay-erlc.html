About Miami Roleplay

How the server started and who's behind it.

Miami Roleplay launched on September 17, 2026, as an ERLC community built around structured, realistic law enforcement, fire & EMS, and DOT roleplay across Liberty County.

The server is run by Vex, who previously founded and owned CSRP, an earlier roleplay community. That experience running a server from the ground up shaped how Miami Roleplay is structured from day one, from department training to how the executive team operates.

Since launch, the server has grown quickly, driven by an experienced ownership team and a community built around consistent, quality roleplay rather than short-term hype.

Applications

Pick a department below and read its requirements before you apply. Every application is reviewed by that department's leadership.

LEO

Law Enforcement

Patrol, traffic enforcement, criminal response

  • Age 13 or older
  • Working microphone required
  • Clean in-game moderation history
  • Pass a short interview
  • Complete field training before solo patrol
  • Active in the Discord server
FIRE/EMS

Fire & EMS

Medical response, rescue, structure fires

  • Age 13 or older
  • Working microphone required
  • Comfortable with medical roleplay scenarios
  • Pass a short interview
  • Complete probationary shift ride-alongs
  • Active in the Discord server
DOT

Department of Transportation

Road work, signage, traffic control

  • Age 12 or older
  • Understands basic traffic control roleplay
  • Reliable attendance during road events
  • Active in the Discord server
STAFF

Staff Team

Server moderation and community management

  • Age 15 or older
  • Prior moderation experience preferred
  • Good standing with no prior bans
  • Available a consistent number of hours weekly
  • Calm, fair judgment under pressure
  • Active in the Discord server
